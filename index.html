Nine Mile at a glance

What they do
Electronic principal trading firm. ETF market making across APAC plus a high-frequency futures desk. They call themselves "market makers, traders and technology builders".
Claim to fame
The only Australian-owned HFT firm, competing with Susquehanna, Optiver, IMC and Citadel Securities.
Founded
2016, Sydney, by traders and software engineers. Nine Mile Financial Pty Ltd, AFSL 518735.
Founder
Morgan Potter, Managing Director ~10 years. Ex-Macquarie Group MD and Goldman Sachs JBWere MD.
Size
Roughly 20-30 people, growing ~40% YoY (LinkedIn). Staff across Australia, Sri Lanka and Hong Kong; the site lists Sydney and Shanghai offices.
HQ
Level 11, 39 Martin Place, Sydney NSW 2000.

Why now

Momentum is real and the site is not telling it. Proof points to reference:

  1. Awarded Best ETF Market Maker for 2026 by the Tokyo Stock Exchange, plus Best Market Maker for CONNEQTOR (May 2026).
  2. Expanded market making into Korea (April 2026) and won HKEX market-making approval (2025). Hiring posts mention European expansion.
  3. First Australian Authorised Participant onboarded to ETPLink, the DLT platform for ETP primary market orders (Feb 2026).
  4. Hiring their first-ever marketing person: a part-time Marketing Lead whose job includes the website, socials and events (posted Aug 2026, Maddie reshared it).
  5. Active hiring for traders, quant traders and software engineers. They compete with Optiver and Citadel for the same talent.

The website refresh is part of going global and winning a talent war. It is not a cosmetic job.

Their site today

nmftrading.com is a WordPress one-pager: deep blue, big type, animated digits. Confident look, thin content.

Screenshots of nmftrading.com captured Oct 6, 2026.

  • No news or proof anywhere: the TSE award, Korea launch and HKEX approval are invisible on the site.
  • No global footprint story: nothing about APAC venues or the European push.
  • Little for ETF issuers, exchanges and partners, even though "valued partners" is in their mission.
  • Careers is a plain jobs list, while recruiting quant and engineering talent is a stated priority.
  • Culture and founder story ("the only Australian-owned HFT") is claimed in one paragraph, not shown.

Your angle

  1. Lead with the United Carriers parallel: freight was an invisible B2B industry too, until the site made 12M+ people watch it. Market making is even more invisible. Same opportunity, bigger stakes.
  2. Frame the site's two jobs: attract quant and engineering talent away from Optiver/Citadel, and give issuers, exchanges and partners institutional confidence as Nine Mile enters Europe.
  3. Pitch the founder story: the only Australian-owned HFT taking on the global giants. That is a narrative site, not a corporate brochure.
  4. Suggest the site becomes the hub for news and awards (TSE, Korea, HKEX). Right now LinkedIn carries everything and the site says nothing.
  5. Propose a discovery phase first: audiences, content, compliance review needs (they hold an AFSL), then design. Quote scope after discovery.
